- [Covenant Gate](https://github.com/ScrappyHub/governance-interface) (command-line instrument): Deterministic policy decision, consent enforcement, and capability authorization engine.
- [HAAI](https://github.com/ScrappyHub/HAAI) (desktop app): AI verification instrument: records, identifies, timestamps, exports and verifies a person's AI conversations and AI-made changes across browsers and desktop apps; monitors for drift; judges nothing.
- [LawVM](https://github.com/ScrappyHub/LawVM) (command-line instrument): Policy-decision verification and recording instrument for Covenant Gate: deterministically re-evaluates, monitors, and records policy decisions so that Covenant Gate's enforcement can be independently proven correct. [Try it in your browser](https://theconstellationnetwork.com/demos/lawvm).

- [Rooted](https://github.com/ScrappyHub/rooted-platform) (platform): Governed discovery, marketplace, community, and procurement platform on Supabase/PostgreSQL.
- [ShadowProfile](https://github.com/ScrappyHub/shadowprofile) (browser extension): Forensic browser-visible tracking and personalization inspector.
- [SoteriaVault](https://github.com/ScrappyHub/soteriavault) (desktop app): Local-first, standalone encrypted privacy vault: encrypted storage spaces, privacy-state transitions (open / idle / sealed) and deterministic, append-only receipts.

## How everything here is built

- **Standalone first.** Each system builds, tests and runs on its own.
- **Evidence over claims.** Outputs are hashed, signed and receipted so anyone can recompute them.
- **Fail closed.** Missing inputs, bad signatures and stale state stop the work and say why.
- **Local first.** Data stays on your machine unless you choose otherwise.
- **Clear boundaries.** Each system states what it owns and what it leaves to others.
